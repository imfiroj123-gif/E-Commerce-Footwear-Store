Our Story

From Passion
to Premium

ZIVO began in a small workshop with a single vision — to create footwear that bridges the gap between luxury craftsmanship and everyday wearability. What started as a passion project in 2021 has grown into a globally recognized premium footwear brand.

Each ZIVO shoe is a collaboration between master craftsmen, avant-garde designers, and materials scientists who push the boundaries of what footwear can be. We don't just make shoes — we engineer experiences.
